FAQ

Career Mentorship FAQs

Who is this career mentorship for?

This is for software engineers and developers who feel stuck — unsure which direction to grow, how to break into a better company, or how to command a higher salary. Whether you are a fresher trying to land your first role or an experienced developer targeting a senior position, we build a personalised roadmap for you.

What does the AI-powered career roadmap include?

The roadmap covers your target role, skill gaps to close, a week-by-week action plan, resume and LinkedIn improvements, and specific companies to target. It is built from your current profile and your career goals — not a generic template.

How is this different from generic career coaching?

Generic coaching gives advice. We give you a concrete plan tied to your specific background, skills, and goals — combined with 1-on-1 mentorship from engineers who have hired and been hired at top tech companies. Every session is action-oriented, not just motivational.

How many mentorship sessions are included?

Session count depends on the plan you choose. Contact us on WhatsApp for the latest plan details and pricing — we keep batch sizes small so every mentee gets focused attention.

Can I combine career mentorship with the AI Engineering or Java Full Stack course?

Yes — many of our students pair a course with mentorship to both skill up and navigate their next career move simultaneously. Reach out on WhatsApp to discuss a combined package.
